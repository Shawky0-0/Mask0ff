## What happened

A function that was only supposed to accept messages relayed from Axelar accepted a message
from whoever sent one. The attacker crafted a message telling the contract to release
tokens, and it released them. Roughly $3 million, February 2026.

## Why it is here next to a $230 million incident

**Because it is the same bug.** Both are "we trusted the caller because of where we assumed
they came from". One lost $230 million and one lost $3 million, and the difference is size,
not sophistication.

Neither needed cryptography, a novel primitive, or a clever gas trick. **Both are
authorisation.**

## The pattern across both web3 entries today

allowed to act.** For a folder that exists to prepare for a possible migration, that is a
